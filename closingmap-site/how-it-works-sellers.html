Before anything else, run the seller net sheet. Enter your asking price, your mortgage payoff balance, and your location. The calculator returns your projected proceeds after commissions, closing costs, transfer taxes, and payoff. Most sellers don’t find out their real net until they’re sitting at the closing table. You can find out now, free, no account required.

Six questions about where you are in the process. Are you selling immediately or still planning? Do you have an agent? What steps have you already taken? Get your readiness score and dashboard

Build your seller profile

Consider the Verified Seller Program

Your seller dashboard shows your readiness score and the full path ahead: listing strategy, inspection, title and escrow, closing preparation, and closing day. Your next recommended step is surfaced prominently. As you complete steps like gathering documents, connecting with your agent, and completing title work, your score advances.

Your document vault collects the papers sellers need at closing: the deed, current property tax bills, existing mortgage information, your prior title policy, any existing survey, a capital improvements list, and utility records. Your team includes your listing agent, your closing attorney, and, if applicable, a title company. The system tells you what’s complete and what’s still outstanding.

Most title issues surface one to two weeks before closing, when there’s no time and no good options. ClosingMap’s Verified Seller Program runs a preliminary title search before you list. If there’s a lien, an outstanding judgment, or a gap in the chain of title, you’ll know while you still have time to fix it. Sellers who complete the process receive a Verified Seller badge that signals transaction-readiness to buyers and their agents.
